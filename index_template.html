SpicyTerminal turns any flight text or screenshot into a perfect GDS ready to copy format to paste into Backoffice.
      Paste a Google Flights option, an airline email, or a photo of a flight option and you get GDS format ready to copy in milliseconds, computed by a deterministic engine that runs entirely inside this tab. BOOKING LINK lights up for a complete, certain itinerary on one bookable airline — American, Delta, Alaska, United or British Airways, one or several legs — using the same link engine as the production Spicy Link Generator. American links pin every flight via aa.com metasearch; Delta and Alaska links carry every leg (with an estimated price); United and British Airways links carry the route(s) and dates without pinning exact flights. A mixed-airline itinerary links only when exactly one of its airlines is bookable, and then covers that airline's legs. Clicking opens an external search or prefilled summary for one adult and shares the itinerary details with that site; unsupported airlines and uncertain conversions stay disabled. Check availability and price on the airline site — it never books or purchases a ticket automatically.

WHAT IT DOES

        
    
          
  • 
            Copy-ready GDS output. Ticket order, 12-hour clocks, overnight +1 markers, real IATA codes — byte-identical every time.
    • 
          
  • 
            Text or screenshots. Paste a Google Flights card, an airline email or a photo — it converts the moment it lands.
    • 
          
  • 
            AI FIX. When a first read cannot see the flights, it re-reads and repairs the output. Your key, your call.
    • 
          
  • 
            Private. Conversion stays in this tab. Clicking BOOKING LINK opens an external airline search or prefilled summary for one adult and shares the itinerary details with that site — flight numbers, routes and dates (American, Delta and Alaska links; Delta and Alaska also carry an estimated price), or routes and dates only (United, British Airways). It never books or purchases automatically; you always finish on the airline site.
