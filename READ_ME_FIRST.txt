 SPICY TERMINAL — TEAM INSTRUCTIONS (WITH PICTURES)
 https://spicyterminal.netlify.app/
====================================================

Open the pictures in order. Each picture is one step.

HOW TO USE (no key needed for text)
-----------------------------------
step01  Open your browser, go to  spicyterminal.netlify.app
        (bookmark it — the whole app is that one page)

step02  First visit only: press ENTER on the welcome screen.

step03  Paste your flights into the left box with Ctrl+V.
        It converts BY ITSELF — no button needed.
        (You can also press CONVERT if you like.)

step04  The perfect GDS itinerary appears on the right.
        Press COPY, then paste into BO with Ctrl+V. Done.

step05  Screenshots work too: paste the picture the same way.
        A tiny preview appears next to the × button —
        that's your proof the picture landed.
        × clears everything. + ATTACH also adds pictures.

AI KEY — ONLY for screenshots / very messy text
(one time, 20 seconds, free, never again)
-----------------------------------------------
step06  Press "Generate Api" (small words, bottom right).

step07  Google opens. Press the blue "Create API key" button.
        (Sign in with any Google account if asked.)

step08  Press the copy icon next to your key.
        (The key is long and starts with AIza...)

step09  Back in SpicyTerminal the key box is already open.
        Paste the key, press SAVE.
        Saved forever in your browser. Never again.

That's it. After this, pictures convert by AI automatically.

SOMETHING WRONG?
----------------
Press "Report a bug" (bottom right) — it emails Lamar
a ready-made report with everything needed to fix it.
